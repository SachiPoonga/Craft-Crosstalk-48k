## Dataset Attribution
No raw audio files are redistributed in this repository. The research checkpoints were trained on mixtures synthesized from the following public datasets:
- **OpenSLR 83** (UK & Ireland English, CC BY-SA 4.0)
- **OpenSLR 65** (Tamil, Apache 2.0)
- **OpenSLR 66** (Telugu, CC BY-SA 4.0)
- **OpenSLR 68** (Mandarin, Non-Commercial Research)
- **OpenSLR 103** (Hindi, Non-Commercial Research)
- **OpenSLR 28** (Room Impulse Responses, Apache 2.0)
- **MUSDB18-HQ** (Vocal-free music stems, Non-Commercial Research)
- **DEMAND 48 kHz** (Environmental noise recordings, CC BY-SA 3.0)
